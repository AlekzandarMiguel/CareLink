The Problem & Our Solution

Transforming Hospital Patient Referrals

How CareLink eliminates communication bottlenecks between referring clinics, specialist centers, and transit teams.

Traditional Referral Challenges

  • Protracted Telephone Queues: Referring clinicians spend hours attempting to contact tertiary hospital triage desks while patient stability declines.
  • Unverified Destination Capacity: Ambulances frequently arrive at overloaded emergency departments, forcing emergency diversion to secondary facilities.
  • Incomplete Clinical Handovers: Verbal phone orders and handwritten paper notes result in missed diagnostic histories, duplicate imaging, and medical errors.
  • Unmonitored Highway Transit: No centralized visibility over patient condition, en-route ambulance milestones, or estimated time of arrival.

The CareLink Coordinated Platform

  • Rapid Electronic Submission: Standardized digital clinical forms capture vital signs, trauma scoring, diagnosis, and care requirements in minutes.
  • Instant Bed & Capability Verification: Real-time matrices indicate whether adult ICU, NICU, cath lab, or neurosurgical suites are actively available.
  • Structured Communication: Digital message exchange and document attachments ensure receiving teams review labs, ECGs, and imaging prior to patient arrival.
  • Synchronized Fleet Telemetry: Milestone progression across unit assignment, patient pickup, transit, and formal emergency bay handover.

Standard Operating Protocol

The 4-Stage Referral Lifecycle

Every transfer follows a standardized digital workflow to ensure patient safety and facility readiness.

Stage 1

Referral Initiation

The attending clinician documents initial vitals, clinical impression, reason for transfer, and uploads supporting lab summaries or diagnostic images into the patient e-referral dossier.

Output: Complete electronic referral record with urgency category.

Stage 2

Triage & Facility Routing

Triage coordinators and the algorithmic matching engine identify the optimal receiving hospital with confirmed specialty services and verified bed capacity.

Output: Targeted transmission to candidate receiving hospital.

Stage 3

Hospital Acceptance

The receiving hospital intake team evaluates the clinical record. Upon acceptance, an inpatient or ICU bed is reserved, admitting staff are alerted, and preparation commences.

Output: Formal transfer acceptance notice and bed reservation.

Stage 4

Transit & Handover

The EMS dispatcher assigns transport. Paramedics log departure, route progress, and arrival. Formal transfer of clinical responsibility occurs at the emergency bay with signature signoff.

Output: Timestamped handover completion and archived audit log.

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently asked questions about patient transfer workflows, bed coordination, and platform features.

What healthcare facilities can use the CareLink platform?

CareLink is designed for community clinics, district hospitals, tertiary medical centers, specialized trauma facilities, and ambulance transport providers that need a modern, coordinated platform for patient transfers and bed management.

How does CareLink prevent emergency ambulance diversions?

Under the CareLink protocol, patient transport cannot be authorized until the receiving medical center explicitly confirms clinical acceptance and bed readiness in the system. Real-time capacity visibility prevents patients from being transported to overloaded emergency departments.

How are clinical notes and diagnostic records transmitted?

Referring medical teams can attach digital documentation (laboratory findings, ECG strips, radiology summaries, and clinical progress notes) directly to the referral dossier. The receiving hospital's clinical team can review these files immediately, formulate a pre-arrival plan, and send guidance back to the referring team.

What happens during an urgent life-threatening emergency?

Referrals flagged with the Resuscitation acuity priority bypass standard waiting queues and trigger high-priority alerts for triage coordinators and emergency intake teams, ensuring immediate specialist review within minutes.

How does a hospital get started on CareLink?

Hospital administrators can submit a facility profile through the registration portal. Once approved by system administrators, staff and dispatch accounts are configured to start submitting and managing referrals immediately.
