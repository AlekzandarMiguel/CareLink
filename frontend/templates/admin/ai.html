Active Engine Capabilities

Autonomous intelligence features

  • Clinical MEWS Vital Scoring: Auto-calculates early warning score (0-14) from systolic BP, HR, RR, temp, and AVPU.
  • In-Transit Fleet Congestion: Dynamically decrements available ICU beds by the number of ambulances en route.
  • Integrated Road ETA: Geodesic waypoint calculation with simulated traffic multipliers.
  • Historical Acceptance Weighting: Prioritizes facilities with demonstrated specialty throughput.
